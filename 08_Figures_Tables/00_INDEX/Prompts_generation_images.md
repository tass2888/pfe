## Chapitre 3 — Butler & Shacklock

**3.1 — `Fig_3.1_Glisseur_neural.png`**
Two-panel side-view schematic of a neural gliding technique (slider). As the shoulder moves in one direction, the elbow straightens in the other direction so the neural bed length stays almost constant while arrows show the nerve excursion sliding through the surrounding tissue. Short labels 'glisseur', 'excursion neurale', 'contrainte faible'. Emphasize coordinated opposite joint movements.

**3.2 — `Fig_3.2_Tenseur_neural.png`**
Two-panel side-view schematic of a neural tensioning technique (tensioner). Shoulder and elbow movements both lengthen the neural bed at the same time, placing the nerve under greater tension. Arrows show increased stretch along the nerve, with subtle muted red at the nerve. Short labels 'tensionneur', 'allongement du lit neural', 'contrainte augmentée'. Visually contrast with a slider: both ends move together.

Short labels 'ULNT1', 'nerf médian', 'abduction', 'supination', 'extension du coude', 'mouvement sensibilisant'. Simple line-art patient, no facial detail.

**3.4 — `Fig_3.4_Tests_median_radial_ulnaire.png`**

Short labels 'interface mécanique', 'foramen', 'ouverture', 'fermeture'.
